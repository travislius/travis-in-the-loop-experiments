# Bramble B4 Robot Lawn Mower — Owner's Manual (v2.3)

Fictional product written for the Travis in the Loop RAG experiment, October 2026. Bramble Garden Robotics does not exist; every fact below is invented so that no language model could have learned it during training.

## 1. What's in the box

The Bramble B4 ships with the mower unit, the Nest charging dock, 180 metres of boundary wire, 300 wire pegs, a spare blade set of six pivoting blades, the dock power adapter, and a quick-start card. The mower weighs 11.4 kilograms without the battery cover. The battery is a 28-volt, 5.2 amp-hour lithium pack.

## 2. Choosing a dock location

Place the Nest dock on flat ground no steeper than 5 degrees, with at least 2 metres of straight boundary wire in front of the dock entrance and 1 metre behind it. The dock must be within 10 metres of an outdoor socket. Do not place the dock in a hollow where rainwater collects.

## 3. Laying the boundary wire

Keep the wire 35 centimetres from hard edges such as walls and fences, and 10 centimetres from flat paths that the mower may cross. Pegs should be placed every 75 centimetres along straight runs and every 30 centimetres on curves. The maximum supported boundary loop length is 450 metres. To create an island around a flowerbed or tree, run both wire legs to and from the island side by side in the same peg so the signals cancel.

## 4. Mowing area and slopes

The Bramble B4 is rated for lawns up to 1,400 square metres. It can climb slopes up to 24 degrees (45 percent). Near the boundary wire, slopes should not exceed 15 degrees, or the mower may slide out of the working area.

## 5. Cutting height

Cutting height is adjusted with the orange dial under the lid, from 22 millimetres to 62 millimetres in 8 steps. For the first mow of the season, set the highest position and lower it by one step per week.

## 6. Schedules and quiet mode

The default schedule mows from 09:00 to 17:00 on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. In the Bramble app, Quiet Mode lowers blade speed from 3,100 rpm to 2,300 rpm and limits operation to 08:00–20:00. Quiet Mode reduces noise from 58 dB(A) to 52 dB(A) but increases mowing time by about 30 percent.

## 7. Rain behaviour

The B4 has a rain sensor on the top cover. When rain is detected, the mower returns to the dock and waits for a rain delay, which is 180 minutes by default and can be set between 30 and 480 minutes. Turning the rain sensor off is not recommended because wet grass clogs the blade disc.

## 8. Lift, tilt and safety

If the mower is lifted, the blades stop within 2 seconds. If it tilts more than 35 degrees, the blades also stop. The Child Lock requires a four-digit PIN before any button press takes effect. The default PIN is 0000 and must be changed during setup. After three wrong PIN entries, the keypad locks for 10 minutes.

## 9. Indicator light codes

The status light on the lid uses colours and blink patterns:
- Solid green: charging complete, ready.
- Pulsing green: charging.
- Solid blue: mowing.
- Two slow amber blinks, repeated: boundary signal lost. Check that the dock is powered and the wire is unbroken.
- Three fast red blinks, repeated: the blade motor is blocked. Switch off with the main switch, wear gloves, and clear grass or debris from the blade disc.
- Alternating red and blue: firmware update in progress. Do not remove the mower from the dock.
- Solid red: battery temperature outside 0–45 °C. Charging pauses until the battery returns to range.

## 10. Blade replacement

Replace all six blades at the same time, every 8 weeks during the mowing season, or sooner if the cut looks ragged. Use only Bramble BX-6 blades. Each blade is held by one T15 Torx screw; tighten to 1.2 newton-metres. Never sharpen blades; replace them.

## 11. Winter storage

Before winter, charge the battery to 100 percent, switch the mower off with the main switch, and store it indoors between 5 °C and 25 °C. Unplug the dock and cover it, or bring it indoors. Recharge the battery to full every 3 months during storage.

## 12. Firmware updates

Firmware updates are installed over Wi-Fi from the Bramble app while the mower sits in the dock. An update takes about 12 minutes. Firmware version 4.2 added an edge-cut mode that follows the boundary wire once per week at the start of a Monday session.

## 13. Anti-theft

The B4 has a GPS tracker and a theft alarm. If the mower is lifted outside the boundary while the Child Lock is enabled, an 85 dB alarm sounds and the app sends the location. Location tracking requires the Bramble Connect subscription, which costs 2 euros per month after a free first year.

## 14. Warranty

The mower carries a 3-year limited warranty, and the battery is covered for 2 years. The warranty is void if non-BX-6 blades are fitted or if the mower is opened by anyone other than an authorised Bramble service partner. Register within 30 days of purchase to receive one extra year of mower warranty.

## 15. Technical support

Contact Bramble support at the in-app chat, open 08:00 to 18:00 on weekdays. Have the 14-character serial number ready; it is printed under the battery cover and starts with the letters BRB.
